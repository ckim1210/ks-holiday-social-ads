Holiday video ads, in the feeds where people will see them

Swap the video

Drop the campaign files into assets/video/ and point video.vertical (9:16) and video.landscape (16:9) at them in config.js. Details are in the README.

Change the destination

Every tappable brand element reads siteUrl from config.js. Add UTM parameters there to attribute clicks by mockup or session.

Fidelity note

Platform chrome is approximated with text, CSS and hand-drawn icons. No platform logo image files are used.
